# Playbook: Month-to-Month Contract Retention Strategy

## Risk Context
Customers on month-to-month contracts represent our highest churn demographic (~42% churn rate). Without a long-term commitment, these customers encounter zero friction when switching to a competitor.

## Recommended Action Plan
1. **Offer Term Migration Incentives:** Present a 15% to 20% discount on total monthly billing if the customer switches to a 1-year or 2-year contract.
2. **Highlight Annual Savings:** Frame the offer around annual cost savings (e.g., "Save over $200 this year by locking in your rate today").
3. **Waive Upgrade Fees:** Remove any admin or equipment modification fees required to transition from monthly billing to a fixed term.
