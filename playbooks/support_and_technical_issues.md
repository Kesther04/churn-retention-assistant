# Playbook: Resolving Technical Support Friction

## Risk Context
Customers encountering frequent service degradation or unresolved technical tickets are prone to immediate emotional churn, regardless of contract duration.

## Recommended Action Plan
1. **Priority Support Escalation:** Assign a dedicated senior support technician or manager to resolve persistent connectivity issues within 24 hours.
2. **Proactive Outage & Service Credits:** Apply a automatic 1-week service credit to the next billing cycle following any major reported outage or repeated technician visits.
3. **Free Hardware Refresh:** Replace legacy or faulty modems/routers with upgraded equipment free of charge to guarantee optimal performance.
