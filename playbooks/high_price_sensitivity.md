# Playbook: Managing High Price Sensitivity & Bill Shock

## Risk Context
Customers billed over $70 per month—especially those on Fiber Optic plans without bundled add-ons—show significantly higher churn rates due to perceived value imbalance.

## Recommended Action Plan
1. **Conduct a Value Audit:** Review the customer's account for underutilized premium features (e.g., unviewed streaming channels) and offer to strip unused extras to reduce monthly costs.
2. **Apply Loyalty Credit Bundles:** Offer a temporary $10–$15 monthly account credit for 6 months while retaining full core service speeds.
3. **Transition to Alternative Speed Tiers:** Suggest shifting to a tier slightly lower in bandwidth that still satisfies their household usage while dropping the monthly bill below the $70 threshold.
