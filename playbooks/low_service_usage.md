# Playbook: Re-engaging Low Service Usage Customers

## Risk Context
Customers using single services (e.g., Internet only without Online Security, Tech Support, or Backup) show lower brand stickiness and higher willingness to switch providers.

## Recommended Action Plan
1. **Cross-Sell Security & Protection:** Highlight the benefits of adding Online Security or Tech Support at a discounted bundle rate ($2–$5/month extra).
2. **Promote Self-Service Features:** Educate the customer on automated backup, cloud storage, or mobile app features included with their plan that they have not activated.
3. **Free Feature Trial:** Enable a 60-day zero-cost trial of Tech Support to increase product touchpoints and account reliance.
