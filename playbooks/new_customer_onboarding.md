# Playbook: Early Lifecycle Retention (First 90 Days)

## Risk Context
Churn is heavily concentrated within the first 1 to 3 months of customer tenure. Initial onboarding friction, unexpected setup costs, or early service confusion drive immediate cancellations.

## Recommended Action Plan
1. **Proactive Onboarding Check-in:** Schedule an automated or live support check-in at Day 14 to verify installation satisfaction and connection stability.
2. **Complimentary Add-on Trial:** Provide a free 30-day trial of Online Security or Tech Support to demonstrate product value early without upfront cost.
3. **First-Bill Explanation:** Send an interactive guide or email explaining the first bill breakdown to eliminate confusion around activation fees or prorated charges.
