# Playbook: Annual Plan Discounting & Contract Locking

## Risk Context
Contract duration is the single strongest factor in customer retention. Two-year contract customers exhibit less than a 3% overall churn rate.

## Recommended Action Plan
1. **Tiered Term Discounts:** 
   - 1-Year Contract: 10% monthly discount + free speed upgrade.
   - 2-Year Contract: 20% monthly discount + price-freeze guarantee.
2. **Auto-Pay Incentive:** Offer an additional $5 monthly bill credit if the customer enrolls in automatic bank or credit card payments alongside an annual agreement.
3. **Early Renewal Offers:** Target customers reaching month 10 of a 12-month contract with early renewal perks before they begin shopping for competitor deals.
